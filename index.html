FAQ

Common questions

Will it work on our old office computers?

Our tools are designed for Windows 7, 10 and 11. We check it on your actual computer during the free trial.

Do you keep our portal passwords?

No. Logins stay on your computer. If a portal sends an OTP, your team types it in.

What if a portal or SAP screen changes later?

The support plan covers it. We update the tool so your daily work does not stop.

How do we know the results are correct?

Every record is matched by its own ID, every run saves a log, and anything unclear is flagged for a person to review.

How much does it cost?

Projects start from INR 15,000 for one workflow, plus optional monthly support from INR 2,000. We send an exact quote after the free trial.

Can you install it if we are in another city?

Yes. Installation works remotely. You run one installer and we guide you through the rest.
